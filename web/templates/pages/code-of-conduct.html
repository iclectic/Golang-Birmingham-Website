Code of Conduct

A friendly, safe and welcoming community

Golang Birmingham is for everyone. This code of conduct applies to all our events and online spaces, and to everyone who takes part: attendees, speakers, sponsors, volunteers and organisers.

The short version

We are committed to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Be kind, be patient and assume good intent. Harassment of any kind will not be tolerated.

Expected behaviour

  • Be welcoming. Make space for newcomers and people with different levels of experience. There are no stupid questions.
  • Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people. Respect that others may have different views and backgrounds.
  • Be considerate. Think about how your words and actions affect others, including people who aren't in the room.
  • Be collaborative. Share knowledge generously and give credit where it's due.
  • Look out for each other. If you notice someone in distress or being made uncomfortable, check in with them or let an organiser know.

Unacceptable behaviour

  • Offensive comments related to any of the characteristics listed above
  • Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or physical contact
  • Deliberate intimidation, stalking or following
  • Photographing or recording someone who has asked not to be
  • Sustained disruption of talks or other events
  • Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
  • Advocating for, or encouraging, any of the above

Speakers and sponsors are held to the same standard. Talks, slides and stands should be suitable for a professional, mixed audience.

Reporting a problem

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, or have any other concerns, please tell us as soon as possible. You can:

  • Speak to any organiser in person at the event
  • Email {{.Site.ConductEmail}}, which is only seen by the organising team
  • Message an organiser privately on Meetup

Helpful details include what happened, when and where, who was involved and whether there were any witnesses, but please report even if you don't have all of these. All reports are handled in confidence. If a report involves an organiser, they will not be involved in handling it.

Enforcement

Organisers will review every report promptly and fairly. Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behaviour is expected to comply immediately. Depending on the situation, we may:

  • Have a private conversation and issue a warning
  • Ask the person to leave the event, without a refund (our events are free anyway)
  • Remove the person from the Meetup group and ban them from future events
  • Involve venue security or the police where appropriate

We will follow up with the person who made the report to let them know how it was handled, where appropriate.

Scope

This code applies at our meetups, workshops and socials (including at venues and the pub afterwards), and in our online spaces such as Meetup, social media and chat groups. It also applies when someone is representing Golang Birmingham elsewhere.

Attribution
