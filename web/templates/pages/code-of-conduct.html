Code of Conduct

Our guidelines for creating a safe, inclusive, and welcoming environment for all community members.

Our Commitment

Golang Birmingham is dedicated to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or technology choices.

We do not tolerate harassment of participants in any form. Sexual language and imagery is not appropriate for any venue, including talks, workshops, social media, or other community platforms.

This code of conduct applies to all spaces managed by Golang Birmingham, including our meetups, workshops, online communities, and social media channels. We expect all community members to follow these guidelines in all community interactions.

Expected Behavior

Be Respectful

Treat all community members with respect and consideration. Value different viewpoints and experiences. Be respectful when providing feedback and when receiving feedback.

Be Inclusive

Create an inclusive environment where everyone feels welcome. Use inclusive language and be mindful of cultural differences. Make space for others to participate.

Be Collaborative

Work together to build a strong community. Share knowledge freely and help others learn. Focus on what is best for the community as a whole.

Be Professional

Maintain professional conduct in all interactions. Be mindful of your language and behavior. Remember that you represent both yourself and the Golang Birmingham community.

Be Constructive

Provide constructive feedback and criticism. Focus on ideas and code rather than individuals. Help others improve and learn from your experiences.

Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment

Any form of harassment, including but not limited to: offensive comments, verbal threats, intimidation, stalking, photography or recording without consent, sustained disruption of talks or events, and unwelcome physical contact.

Discrimination

Discriminatory jokes, language, or actions based on gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or technology choices.

Inappropriate Content

Sharing or displaying sexually explicit or violent content, unwelcome sexual attention, or any content that makes others uncomfortable.

Disruptive Behavior

Continuing to initiate interaction with someone after being asked to stop, disrupting events or presentations, or otherwise interfering with the ability of others to participate.

Event-Specific Guidelines

During Talks and Presentations

  • Listen attentively and respect the speaker
  • Save questions for the designated Q&A session
  • Avoid side conversations during presentations
  • Be mindful of recording devices and photography policies
  • Respect time limits during Q&A sessions

During Networking and Social Events

  • Respect personal space and boundaries
  • Ask before taking photos of others
  • Be inclusive in conversations and group activities
  • Remember that professional boundaries still apply
  • Look out for others who may seem uncomfortable or isolated

Online and Social Media

  • Be respectful in all online interactions
  • Don't share private information without consent
  • Report inappropriate content to organizers
  • Remember that community guidelines apply online too
  • Think before you post - once online, always online

Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this code of conduct, please report it to the event organizers or community moderators. All reports will be handled confidentially and investigated promptly.

Email

Send an email to {{.Site.ContactEmail}} with the subject "Code of Conduct Report". Please include as much detail as possible about the incident.

In Person

Speak with any of the event organizers, who will be wearing identifying badges or shirts. You can ask to speak with them privately.

Anonymous Reporting

If you prefer to report anonymously, you can use our anonymous reporting form or leave a note with any organizer.

What to Include in Your Report

  • Your contact information (optional, for follow-up)
  • Date, time, and location of the incident
  • Description of the behavior or incident
  • Names of individuals involved (if known)
  • Any witnesses to the incident
  • Any evidence or supporting documentation

Enforcement

Community organizers and moderators are responsible for enforcing this code of conduct. They have the right and responsibility to take appropriate action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Possible Actions

  • A private warning or conversation about the behavior
  • A request for a public or private apology
  • Temporary removal from the event or community space
  • Permanent ban from future events and community spaces
  • Involvement of law enforcement if necessary

Our Approach

  • We will respond to all reports promptly and thoroughly
  • We will respect privacy and confidentiality when handling reports
  • We will provide support to those who experience harassment
  • We will follow due process and consider all perspectives
  • We will document all incidents and actions taken

Contact Information

If you have questions about this code of conduct or need to report a concern, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.

Email: {{.Site.ContactEmail}}
Response Time: We aim to respond to all reports within 24 hours
Confidentiality: All reports will be handled with the utmost confidentiality
