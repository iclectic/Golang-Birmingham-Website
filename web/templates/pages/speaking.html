Speaking

Speak at Golang Birmingham

Our meetups are powered by the community. If you've built something, learned something or broken something with Go, people here want to hear about it. First-time speakers are especially welcome.

Ideas

What could I talk about?

Anything that would interest a room of Go developers. Talks don't need to be advanced. Some of our most popular talks have been beginner-friendly introductions.

  • Your first Go project
  • Concurrency
  • Testing & fuzzing
  • Web services & APIs
  • Generics
  • Performance & profiling
  • Cloud & Kubernetes
  • CLIs & tooling
  • Go in production
  • Open source
  • Security
  • Career stories

What we'll do for you

  • Help you shape your idea and abstract
  • Pair you with an organiser for a practice run
  • Provide a projector, mic and HDMI/USB-C adapters
  • Promote your talk on Meetup and social media
  • Record it (only if you're happy for us to)
  • Feed you pizza afterwards

How it works

From idea to stage in three steps

  1. Send us your idea

    A title and a couple of sentences is plenty. It doesn't need to be polished.

  2. We'll have a chat

    An organiser gets back to you within a week to talk it through and find a date.

  3. Give your talk

    We'll announce it, help you prepare and make sure everything works on the night.
