Ways to support the community

Every bit helps, and we're happy to tailor something that works for you.

Host a meetup

Space for 40–80 people, a screen or projector and wifi. City-centre venues near New Street, Snow Hill or Moor Street are ideal.

{{template "icon-pizza"}}

Food & drink

Cover pizza and soft drinks for an evening and get a shout-out from the stage and on Meetup.

{{template "icon-video"}}

Help us record and publish talks so the whole Go community can learn from Birmingham's speakers.
