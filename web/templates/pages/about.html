About Golang Birmingham

Learn about our mission, community, and what we do for Go developers in Birmingham.

Our Mission

Golang Birmingham is a community-driven group dedicated to bringing together Go developers, software engineers, and technology enthusiasts in Birmingham and the surrounding areas.

We create a welcoming environment where people can learn from each other, share knowledge, and grow their skills in Go programming and software development.

Whether you're an experienced Go developer, just starting your journey, or simply curious about the language, you'll find a place in our community.

What We Do

Technical Talks

Monthly talks on Go programming, software architecture, best practices, and real-world applications from local and international speakers.

Hands-on Workshops

Practical sessions where you can code alongside experienced developers, learn new techniques, and build real projects.

Community Networking

Opportunities to connect with other developers, share experiences, and build lasting professional relationships.

Knowledge Sharing

A platform for sharing ideas, discussing challenges, and learning from the collective experience of our community members.

Who Should Join

Go Developers

Backend Engineers

Cloud & DevOps

Students & Learners

Tech Enthusiasts

People curious about Go, programming languages, and technology trends.

Open Source Contributors

Developers passionate about open source and collaborative development.

Inclusivity

Community Guidelines

Golang Birmingham is committed to creating a safe, inclusive, and welcoming environment for all participants. We expect all community members to:

  • Treat everyone with respect and consideration
  • Be open to different perspectives and experiences
  • Help create a welcoming and inclusive environment
  • Focus on what is best for the community
  • Show empathy towards other community members

For detailed guidelines, please read our Code of Conduct.

Important: Golang Birmingham is an independently organised community group and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or the Go project. We are a community-driven initiative run by volunteers who are passionate about Go programming and building a strong tech community in Birmingham.
