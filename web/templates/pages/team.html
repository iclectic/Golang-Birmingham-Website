Volunteer With Us

Golang Birmingham is run entirely by volunteers who are passionate about building a strong Go community in Birmingham. We're always looking for people to help with:

Event Organisation

Help plan and coordinate our monthly meetups and special events.

Speaker Coordination

Reach out to potential speakers and help manage our speaking schedule.

Community Management

Manage our online presence and engage with the community.

Technical Support

Help with our website, streaming, and other technical needs.

Content Creation

Write blog posts, create social media content, and document our events.

Sponsorship Outreach

Help us find and work with sponsors to support our community.

Interested in volunteering?

We'd love to have you join our team! No specific experience is required - just enthusiasm for Go and our community.

Our Values

As a team, we're committed to:

Inclusivity

Creating a welcoming environment for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

Community First

Making decisions that benefit our community and help it grow sustainably.

Transparency

Being open about how we operate and making decisions collaboratively.

Continuous Learning

Encouraging learning and growth for both our team and our community.
