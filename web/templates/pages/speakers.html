Speaking Guidelines

Talk Formats

  • Standard Talks: 30-45 minutes including Q&A
  • Lightning Talks: 5-10 minutes on a specific topic
  • Workshops: 2-3 hour hands-on sessions
  • Panels: Group discussions on specific topics

Topic Suggestions

  • Go language features and techniques
  • Real-world project case studies
  • Performance optimization strategies
  • Testing and debugging approaches
  • Microservices and distributed systems
  • DevOps and deployment practices
  • Career development in Go

What We Provide

  • Projector and screen
  • Microphone and sound system
  • Video recording (with your permission)
  • Event promotion on our channels
  • Supportive audience and feedback
  • Refreshments and networking opportunities
