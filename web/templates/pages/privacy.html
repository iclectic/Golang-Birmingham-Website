Privacy Policy

How Golang Birmingham collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Our Privacy Commitment

Golang Birmingham is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with our community, attend our events, or use our website.

This policy applies to all information collected by Golang Birmingham, including through our website, event registrations, email communications, and social media platforms.

By participating in our community or attending our events, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Name and contact details (email address, phone number)
  • Professional information (job title, company)
  • Demographic information (age range, location)
  • Communication preferences

Event-Related Information

  • Event registration details
  • Attendance records
  • Feedback and survey responses
  • Workshop participation

Technical Information

  • IP address and browser information
  • Device information
  • Website usage and interaction data
  • Cookies and similar technologies

Communications

  • Email correspondence
  • Social media interactions
  • Community forum participation
  • Contact form submissions

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

Event Management

To organize and manage events, send event updates, and communicate with attendees.

Community Engagement

To facilitate community interactions, connect members, and enhance the community experience.

Communication

To send newsletters, event announcements, and relevant community updates.

Website Improvement

To analyze usage patterns, improve our website, and enhance user experience.

Safety and Security

To ensure event safety, enforce community guidelines, and protect our members.

Legal Compliance

To comply with legal obligations and protect our organization's rights.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

Community Directory

With your consent, we may include your name and professional information in our community directory.

Event Partners

We may share attendee information with venue partners and sponsors for event logistics purposes.

Service Providers

We may share information with third-party service providers who help us operate our events and website.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. Our security measures include:

  • Secure storage of personal information
  • Encryption of sensitive data
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
  • Access controls and authentication systems
  • Staff training on data protection
  • Regular security audits and assessments

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Access

Request access to the personal information we hold about you.

Correction

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.

Deletion

Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal obligations.

Portability

Request a copy of your information in a portable format.

Objection

Object to the processing of your information for certain purposes.

Restriction

Request restriction of how your information is processed.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience and analyze website usage. Types of cookies we use include:

Essential Cookies

Required for the website to function properly.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors use our website.

Functional Cookies

Remember your preferences and settings.

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Note that disabling cookies may affect your experience on our website.

Third-Party Services

Our website and events may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies. These include:

  • Event registration platforms (Meetup, Eventbrite)
  • Email marketing services
  • Social media platforms
  • Analytics services
  • Payment processors (for paid events)

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party services, as we are not responsible for their data practices.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 16, we will take steps to delete such information immediately.

If you believe we have collected information from a child under 16, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or business operations. When we make changes, we will:

  • Post the updated policy on our website
  • Update the "Last Updated" date
  • Notify our community of significant changes
  • Obtain consent where required by law

Your continued use of our services after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated privacy policy.
