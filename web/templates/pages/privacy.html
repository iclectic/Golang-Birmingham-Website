Privacy

Privacy notice

The short version: this website doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking, and we don't sell or share your data.

This website

This site does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Our web server keeps short-lived technical logs (such as the page requested, time and IP address) to keep the site secure and running. These are not used to identify you.

Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when loading them. See Google's privacy policy.

Meetup.com

Event RSVPs and group membership are managed on Meetup.com. When you join or RSVP, Meetup's own privacy policy applies. Organisers can see your Meetup profile name and RSVP, and may share attendee names with a venue where it's needed for building security.

Photos and recordings

We sometimes take photos and record talks at our events. Speakers are always asked before being recorded. If you'd prefer not to appear in photos, let an organiser know and we'll make sure you're left out.

Contacting us

If you email us, we'll only use your details to reply. To ask about any data we hold about you, email {{.Site.ContactEmail}}.
