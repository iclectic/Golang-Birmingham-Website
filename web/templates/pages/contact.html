Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I receive a response?

We aim to respond to all enquiries within 2-3 business days. For urgent matters, please mention this in your message.

Do I need to be an experienced Go developer to join?

Not at all! We welcome people of all experience levels, from complete beginners to seasoned professionals. Our community is inclusive and supportive.

Are events free to attend?

Most of our events are free to attend thanks to our sponsors and volunteers. Some special workshops or events may have a small fee to cover costs.

How can I become a speaker?

We'd love to have you speak! You can submit a talk proposal through our speaker form, or contact us directly if you have questions about speaking opportunities.

Can I sponsor Golang Birmingham?

Yes! We offer various sponsorship opportunities to support our community. Please contact us with "Sponsorship Enquiry" in the subject line to learn more.
