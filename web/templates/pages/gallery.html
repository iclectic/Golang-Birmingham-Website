Photo Credits

Photos are taken by community members and event photographers. If you have photos from our events that you'd like to share, please get in touch with us!

By attending Golang Birmingham events, you consent to being photographed for use in our community gallery and promotional materials. If you prefer not to be photographed, please let us know.

Submit Your Photos

Photo Guidelines

For Photographers

  • Focus on capturing the community atmosphere and learning moments
  • Be respectful of attendees' privacy and preferences
  • Avoid taking photos during sensitive discussions or presentations
  • Share high-quality images (minimum 1200px width)
  • Include event name and date in your submission

For Attendees

  • Feel free to take photos during networking and social moments
  • Please avoid photography during presentations unless explicitly permitted
  • Respect others' privacy - ask before taking close-up photos
  • Share your photos with us using our community hashtag
  • Let us know if you prefer not to be photographed
{{end}}
